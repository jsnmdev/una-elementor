Common questions

Before your first visit

What should I bring?

Photo ID, insurance information, medication list, and any relevant dental records you have.

How long should I plan for?

New patient visits are typically longer than routine follow-up appointments.

Will I get treatment the same day?

It depends on the need, diagnosis, time available, and treatment complexity.
