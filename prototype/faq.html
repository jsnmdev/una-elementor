New patients

Before your first visit

Start here if you are joining the practice or returning after a long break.

New patient guide →
What should I bring?

Photo ID, insurance information, medication list, and relevant records if you have them.

How long is the first visit?

New patient appointments are generally longer than routine follow-ups so the team can review your health and priorities.

Will treatment happen the same day?

It depends on urgency, diagnosis, available time, and treatment complexity.

Do you accept my insurance?

Coverage depends on the specific plan. Contact us with your carrier and plan details so the team can help verify benefits.

Can you tell me exactly what insurance pays?

We can estimate based on available plan information, but the carrier makes the final benefit determination.

Do you offer financing?

Financing options may be available depending on treatment and eligibility.
