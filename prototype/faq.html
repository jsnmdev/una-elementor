Questions answered

What patients ask us most.

Quick answers about appointments, insurance, treatment, emergencies, and what to expect at our office.

Appointments

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes, new patient appointments are available based on current schedule capacity.

Can I book online?

You can request an appointment online, and the office will confirm the final time.

What should I bring?

Photo ID, insurance information, medication list, and relevant dental records if available.

Insurance & payment

Do you accept insurance?

We work with many dental plans. Benefits and network status depend on your specific plan.

Do you offer payment plans?

Financing options may be available for qualifying treatment.

Treatment

Do you treat children?

Family dentistry services are available depending on age, needs, and provider scope.

Do you offer cosmetic dentistry?

Yes, cosmetic options may include whitening, bonding, veneers, and alignment depending on your needs.

How often should I visit?

Recall timing depends on gum health, decay risk, restorative history, and other factors.

Emergencies

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call the office for urgent pain, swelling, broken teeth, or dental trauma so the team can help determine the next step.

What if I have facial swelling or trouble breathing?

Seek urgent medical care. Some infections can become serious quickly.

Contact the team directly.
