Ownership rules

Base owns:

H1–H6 and Body. Do not duplicate those as classes.

These classes own:

Recurring typographic presentation such as display, lead, supporting, metadata, and contextual link roles.

They do not own:

Section spacing, layout, surface/background color, component spacing, or arbitrary page-specific typography.

Font calibration:

Exact line-height, weight, and tracking remain calibrated to the actual project fonts at Foundation level.
