How insurance works

Your plan is a benefit, not a treatment plan.

Coverage rules are set by the insurance plan. Clinical recommendations are based on your diagnosis and treatment needs.

We can help with

  • Verifying active benefits
  • Submitting claims
  • Estimating patient responsibility
  • Explaining common plan limitations

Treatment priorities

Plan the care and the cost together.

Good financial conversations should support clinical priorities, not obscure them.

Urgent care
