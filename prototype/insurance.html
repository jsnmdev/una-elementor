The important distinction

Your insurance plan is a benefit. It is not your treatment plan.

Coverage rules come from the carrier. Clinical recommendations come from diagnosis, risk, priorities, and reasonable treatment options.

Estimate ≠ guarantee

We can help estimate coverage based on available information, but the insurance carrier makes the final benefit determination.

Financial FAQ

Do you accept my insurance?

Coverage depends on your specific plan. Contact us with your carrier and plan details.

Can you tell me exactly what insurance will pay?

We can estimate based on available information, but the carrier makes the final determination.

Do you offer financing?

Financing options may be available depending on treatment and eligibility.

Can treatment be phased?

Sometimes. The clinical sequence depends on urgency, health, and what can safely wait.
