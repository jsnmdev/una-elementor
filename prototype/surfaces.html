Surface classes

Surfaces own background and the default foreground context. They do not own spacing, layout, buttons, cards, or component styling. In production these are Elementor Global Classes backed by UNA color variables.

Ownership rules

Surface owns: background color and default foreground/text context.
Surface does not own: section padding, grid, radius, button appearance, media treatment, or card styling.
Elementor production: configure the background and foreground behavior in Elementor, reference UNA Variables, save as a Global Class, and avoid per-section color overrides.
QA gate: headings, paragraphs, links, and later components must remain readable when placed on every approved surface.
