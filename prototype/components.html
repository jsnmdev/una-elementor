Interactive Component

FAQ

Native disclosure behavior first. Later Elementor implementation should preserve keyboard and semantic behavior.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. The office can help verify benefits and explain estimated patient responsibility before treatment.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Your first appointment typically includes a conversation about your goals, an exam, imaging when needed, and a clear treatment discussion.

What if I am nervous about dental treatment?

Tell the team before treatment begins. The visit can be paced around your comfort and questions.

Component Rule

When does something become a component?

If the same structure or content responsibility repeats and should update consistently, make it a component. One-off compositions stay built from Foundation classes and local content.
