Privacy policy.

A readable long-form template for how the website handles forms, analytics, cookies, and basic site information.

Information we collect

This reference page demonstrates the content structure. A production policy must reflect the actual website, analytics, forms, vendors, integrations, and legal requirements in use.

Forms

General website forms should request only information needed for the stated purpose. Sensitive patient information should not be collected through a general marketing form unless the production workflow is specifically designed and approved for it.

Analytics and cookies

Analytics, advertising, call tracking, and cookie technologies should be documented based on the tools actually deployed. The policy should not claim tracking is absent when measurement tools are active.

Third-party services

Scheduling, financing, maps, forms, call tracking, and other embedded services may process information under their own terms and policies.

Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used for website systems and integrations, but no internet transmission can be represented as risk-free.

Policy updates

The policy should be reviewed when the website adds or removes meaningful forms, analytics, advertising, scheduling, or third-party services.

Contact

Questions about website privacy can be directed to the practice through the published contact information.

Production reminder

Policy follows implementation.

Do not copy generic privacy claims without verifying what the live website actually collects and sends.
