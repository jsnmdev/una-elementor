How care is planned

The procedure is not the starting point. The diagnosis is.

Every service should answer four questions: what is happening, why it matters, what options exist, and what should happen next.

  1. Evaluate

    Understand symptoms, health history, and clinical findings.

  2. Prioritize

    Separate urgent needs from elective or monitorable concerns.

  3. Choose

    Compare reasonable treatment paths, tradeoffs, timing, and maintenance.

  4. Maintain

    Protect the result with prevention and follow-up.
