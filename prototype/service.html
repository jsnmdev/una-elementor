What are dental implants?

A stable replacement for missing teeth.

Implants replace the tooth root with a biocompatible post that supports a crown, bridge, or denture.

The goal is to restore function and confidence while protecting surrounding teeth and bone where possible.

Stable function

Implants can provide strong support for chewing when properly planned and maintained.

Natural appearance

The visible restoration is designed to blend with neighboring teeth.

Bone support

Implants can help preserve bone in the area of a missing tooth.

Long-term planning

Treatment is planned around the whole bite, not only the missing space.

Who may be a candidate

Implants are not the right answer for everyone.

Good planning considers health history, bone, gum health, bite forces, expectations, and maintenance.

We evaluate

  • Bone volume and anatomy
  • Gum and periodontal health
  • Medical history and medications
  • Smoking and healing risk
  • Bite and restorative space

The process

What treatment can look like.

    Consultation & imaging

Alternatives

Sometimes another option makes more sense.

Depending on the situation, a bridge, partial denture, complete denture, or no immediate replacement may be reasonable.

Bridge

Uses neighboring teeth for support.

Removable option

Can replace one or multiple missing teeth.

Monitor

In select cases, treatment may be deferred.

Combined treatment

Implants can also support larger restorations.

Common questions

Dental implant FAQ

How long does implant treatment take?

Timing varies based on healing, bone needs, and the type of final restoration.

Does implant placement hurt?

Local anesthesia is used during treatment, and post-operative discomfort varies by case.

How long do implants last?

Longevity depends on health, hygiene, bite forces, maintenance, and other individual factors.

Will insurance cover implants?

Coverage varies by plan. Our team can help review available benefits.
