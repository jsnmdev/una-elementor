UNA-Elementor / Forms

Gravity Forms stays scoped.

Forms are the intentional exception to the Elementor-settings-first rule. Gravity Forms generates its own internal markup, so production styling is centralized in the child theme under .gf-form, while still consuming UNA Variables.

Scope:

Every production Gravity Forms override must begin under .gf-form. Do not globally target every input or Gravity Forms selector across WordPress.

Variables:

Spacing, radii, colors, and typography consume the same UNA Variables as the rest of the site.

Choice controls:

Radio and checkbox groups use semantic fieldsets/legends, keep the native control recognizable, provide generous label hit areas, and retain visible keyboard focus.

Accessibility:

Labels remain visible, required state is communicated, focus is obvious, errors include text, and fields remain comfortable touch targets.

Responsive:

Two-column fields stack to one column on mobile. The submit action becomes full-width where space is constrained.

Boundary:

Elementor controls the section/container around the form. The child-theme form scope controls Gravity Forms internal markup.
